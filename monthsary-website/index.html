Chapter three · all the tomorrows

Our Somewhere 🌙

I don't need every tomorrow figured out. I just love the thought of finding our way through them together.

🏡 A place of our own Love, I want a place for us where it’s just us, our own little world with no interference from the world. I think of it every day: you and me living in a quiet place, away from chaos, where there is less noise and no one to disturb us and one day I will make this dream true for us.
🚆 Places we'll go There are a lot of places on our bucket list, starting with exploring our countries, then Disney and Amsterdam. I want to take you to every place you have mentioned, especially to the castles where you can wear the princess dress and go on exploring the castle, carry you inside the castle and make you feel like a princess. So many dreams that I want to fulfil, and I will try my best to make each one true and make you happy.
☕ Ordinary days I keep imagining the days when there will be no distance, and we will live together. Starting my morning by looking at your pretty face, eating together, playing games together, going for shopping, teasing each other, and growing together. I don’t have words to express how much I want to be with you. When we meet, maybe I will just hug you for a long time, not willing to let you go. Each day is so long to spend without you by my side. I hope this distance ends soon and I don’t have to live away from you ever.

Whatever the next chapter looks like, I pray we keep choosing each other and making it ours together.
